# CRMEF teacher-training seats, Morocco (2024/2025)

Seats opened by the Ministry of National Education for the entrance competitions of the regional teacher-training centres (CRMEF, المراكز الجهوية لمهن التربية والتكوين), 2024/2025 training year: **14,450 seats** (primary 4,052, lower secondary 5,774, upper secondary 4,624).

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `crmef_seats_by_specialty_2024_2025.csv` | Seats by cycle and specialty (`cycle_ar`, `cycle_fr`, `specialty_ar`, `seats`) |
| `crmef_seats_by_region_college_2024_2025.csv` | Lower-secondary seats by region and specialty (non-zero cells only) |
| `crmef_seats_by_region_lycee_2024_2025.csv` | Upper-secondary seats by region and specialty (non-zero cells only) |
| `crmef_seats_history.csv` | Seats by specialty: December 2022, December 2023, 2024/2025 (secondary cycles merged so the years compare) |

- **Official source:** Annex 1 of the ministry's decision opening the CRMEF competitions (2024/2025); December 2022 figures from the 12 regional academies' notices on emploi-public.ma; December 2023 from Annex 1 of that session's notice.
- **Compiled and maintained by Wadifa Info:** https://www.wadifa-info.com/مناصب-مباراة-التعليم-حسب-التخصص (French: https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/postes-concours-enseignement-par-specialite)

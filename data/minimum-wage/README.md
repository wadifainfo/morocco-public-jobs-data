# Morocco minimum wage history: SMIG and SMAG, 1971–2026

Every change of Morocco's legal minimum wage since the single national rate of 1971: the hourly industrial/commercial minimum (SMIG) and the daily agricultural minimum (SMAG), each row tied to its decree and Bulletin officiel issue.

- **Rows:** 35 effective dates, 1971-11-22 to 2026-04-01
- **Latest:** SMIG 17.92 MAD/hour (3422.72 MAD for 191 hours) from 1 January 2026; SMAG 97.44 MAD/day from 1 April 2026 (decree 2-25-983).
- **Sources:** each row links the Bulletin officiel PDF on sgg.gov.ma.
- **Monthly column:** a mechanical hourly × 191 computation (the post-2004 44-hour convention). Before the Labour Code came into force in June 2004 the legal week was 48 hours, so for older rows it is not the legal monthly minimum of the time.
- **Pages:** https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/historique-smig-maroc (history with step chart; Arabic: https://www.wadifa-info.com/تاريخ-الحد-الأدنى-للأجور-بالمغرب) · https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/smig-maroc (current SMIG)

## Columns

`effective_date`, `smig_hourly_mad`, `smig_monthly_191h_mad`, `smag_daily_mad`, `decree`, `bo_number`, `bo_date`, `source_url`, `notes` (blank SMAG = sex-differentiated agricultural rates of the time, see notes).

The full Arabic text of **Organic Law n° 113.14 on communes** (promulgated by dahir 1.15.85 of 7 July 2015, Bulletin officiel (Arabic) n° 6380 of 23 July 2015), as clean machine-readable text: **283 articles**, one JSON row per article with its heading path (part / title / chapter).

Useful for Arabic legal NLP, retrieval and question answering over Moroccan public law, and for exam preparation (articles 77–83 on the commune's powers, 115–118 on administrative control, 121–125 on petitions, 271–274 on governance and audit).

## Source and completeness
Extracted from the Ministry of Justice legal portal (adala.justice.gov.ma), checked against the scanned Bulletin officiel n° 6380 (the law ends at article 283). Editorial footnotes of the portal were removed. This is **the 2015 text as published**; later amendments are not consolidated. Article 51's last paragraph was declared unconstitutional (Constitutional Council decision 968.15) and is not part of the text.

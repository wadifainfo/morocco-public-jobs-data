# Morocco religious holiday dates, 2000–2026 (with the Saudi date for comparison)

The dates on which Morocco actually observed its Islamic holidays, 2000–2026, next to the Saudi (Umm al-Qura) date, because Morocco fixes each month by its own moon sighting and is often one day later.

- **Rows:** 149; holidays: eid_al_adha, eid_al_fitr, extra_day_off, islamic_new_year, mawlid, ramadan_start, unity_day, yennayer
- **Columns:** `gregorian_date`, `hijri_date`, `holiday`, `days_off`, `saudi_date`, `offset_days_vs_saudi`, `confidence`, `source_url`
- **Sources:** Ministry of Habous / SNRT / MAP announcements for recent years; the ICOP sighting archive (astronomycenter.net) for older years. `confidence` says which.
- **Also included:** Amazigh New Year (Yennayer, 14 January, paid holiday since 2024), Unity Day (31 October, from 2026), Ramadan start dates and one-off extra days off.
- **Pages:** https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/dates-fetes-religieuses-maroc-historique (archive) · https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/jours-feries-maroc (current year)

Use it for date conversions, payroll and leave calendars, or to check whether a Morocco date in another dataset was shifted to the Saudi calendar.

# Wadifa Info data studies (2026-10-08)

DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23241447](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23241447)

Four small tables behind the studies published on wadifa-info.com. Figures are frozen at the publication date so that citations stay valid.

| File | Study page | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| `public_posts_per_100k_by_region_2024_2026.csv` | [Emploi public par région](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/emploi-public-par-region) | Concours published on Wadifa Info from 2024-08-01 to 2026-10-07, one row per concours, single-region concours only; legal population RGPH 2024 (HCP) |
| `entry_net_salary_by_diploma_2026.csv` | [Salaire par diplôme](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/salaire-par-diplome-fonction-publique) | Reference grade per diploma; net salary at the 1st échelon from the official simulator simulation.mmsp.gov.ma (single, highest residence zone) |
| `recruitment_fragmentation_2012_2025.csv` | [Statistiques du recrutement](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/statistiques-recrutement-fonction-publique-maroc) | data.gov.ma "statistiques portail emploi public" (ODbL), State + local authorities + public establishments |
| `recruitment_2026_vs_2025_jan_sep.csv` | same page | Concours published on Wadifa Info, January-September of each year |

Limits: concours that do not go through the public-jobs portal are not included; national concours (about 72% of posts) are not assigned to regions; salaries are indicative, not official entitlements.

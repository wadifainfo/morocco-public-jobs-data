# Morocco: budget posts created by each finance law (2020–2027)

Number of new civil-service budget posts (*postes budgétaires*, المناصب المالية) created each year by Morocco's finance law, with a breakdown by department for 2023–2027.

- **Totals:** 2020 to 2027, from the "création de postes budgétaires" article of each finance law (Bulletin officiel).
- **By department:** 11 largest lines of the article, 2023–2027.
- **2027:** draft finance law n° 60.26, tabled on 23 October 2026: **34,454 posts** (Interior 14,200, Defence 5,500, Health 5,400). Marked `bill` until the law is published.
- **Scope:** general state budget only. Excludes the regional education academies (AREF) — 15,590 extra teacher posts in the 2027 bill — and the territorial health groups (2,600 in 2027).
- **Not Morocco:** the figure of 125,675 posts circulating for "PLF 2027" is Algeria's draft budget.

Source page with tables, method and citation: https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/postes-budgetaires-maroc (Arabic: https://www.wadifa-info.com/المناصب-المالية)

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `table` | `total` (national total) or `department` |
| `year` | Finance-law year |
| `department_fr`, `department_ar` | Department (French / Arabic) |
| `posts` | Budget posts created |
| `status` | `law` = adopted finance law, `bill` = draft bill figure |
| `source` | Article used |

## Limits

- A budget post is an authorisation to hire, not a recruitment exam of the same size in the same year.
- 2022 and 2025 department values come from the bill; the adopted 2025 total is 28,906.
- The national police (DGSN) has no separate line in the article.

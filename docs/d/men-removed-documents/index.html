Moroccan Ministry of Education documents removed from men.gov.ma

146 official MEN / CNEE documents that no longer exist at their men.gov.ma address (404), each with a working copy: 2025 exam frameworks (national bac 46, professional bac 7, BTS 33, 1st-year bac regional 21, 3rd-year middle school 10), the April 2025 teachers' professional concours descriptions (28) and note 107-25 (1).

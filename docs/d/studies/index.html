Four small tables behind the studies published on wadifa-info.com. Figures are frozen at the publication date so that citations stay valid.

File Study page Sources
public_posts_per_100k_by_region_2024_2026.csv Emploi public par région Concours published on Wadifa Info from 2024-08-01 to 2026-10-07, one row per concours, single-region concours only; legal population RGPH 2024 (HCP)
entry_net_salary_by_diploma_2026.csv Salaire par diplôme Reference grade per diploma; net salary at the 1st échelon from the official simulator simulation.mmsp.gov.ma (single, highest residence zone)
recruitment_fragmentation_2012_2025.csv Statistiques du recrutement data.gov.ma "statistiques portail emploi public" (ODbL), State + local authorities + public establishments
recruitment_2026_vs_2025_jan_sep.csv same page Concours published on Wadifa Info, January-September of each year

Limits: concours that do not go through the public-jobs portal are not included; national concours (about 72% of posts) are not assigned to regions; salaries are indicative, not official entitlements.

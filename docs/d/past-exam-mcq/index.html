Moroccan civil-service and teacher exam MCQs (real past papers)

1,575 multiple-choice questions from 44 real past papers of Moroccan public recruitment and teacher exams: 829 in Arabic, 686 in French, 60 in English. Questions and options are copied as printed; the answer comes only from the published correction (site, 1,366), an official key printed on the paper (official, 28), or is marked probable (181) when the correction hedges.

Column Meaning
question_id, paper_id Stable ids
paper_title, language, subject, number_on_paper Source paper and topic
question Stem as printed
options Options separated by \|
answer_indices 0-based indices of the correct option(s), space-separated
multiple_answers, negative_marking Marking rules
answer_source official / site / probable
explanation Short explanation from the correction
quiz_url, paper_url Interactive quiz and scanned paper with correction
